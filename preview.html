How Reel Library Works

01

Share from Instagram

Browse Instagram normally. When you spot a reel worth saving, tap the paper airplane Share icon and select Reel Library from your apps list.

02

AI Transcribes & Summarizes

Reel Library extracts the audio track, runs speech-to-text recognition via Whisper, and synthesizes key insights, takeaways, and tags in seconds.

03

Search & Auto-Sync

Every reel is searchable by keyword or category offline. Daily at midnight, your updated vault is archived to your private Google Drive automatically.
